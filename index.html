The Indian culinary treasure

One dish, one memory, one bite at a time.

Kerala is a narrow green strip of coast and backwater at the southern tip of India. Its kitchen is unlike the north's: lighter, sourer, built on coconut milk and curry leaves rather than cream and tomato. That is what we cook here.

Sadya

The feast on a banana leaf

Rice, sambar, avial, thoran, kalan, pickle and payasam, served in the order they are meant to be eaten. The centrepiece of every Kerala celebration.

From the coast

Fish, coconut, kudampuli

Meen curry simmered with gambooge, meen pollichathu wrapped in banana leaf, prawn moilee pale with coconut milk. Sourced fresh, never frozen.

All day

Breakfast until late

Appam with stew, puttu with kadala, idiyappam, dosa from the griddle. Kerala eats these at any hour, and so do we.
