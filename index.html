Our story

Kerala, cooked the way it is at home.

Kairali is a family kitchen in Royal Oak. We cook the food of the Malabar coast and the backwaters — the sadya served on a banana leaf, fish landed and fried the same day, appam with a coconut stew that has been on the stove since morning.

Nothing is pre-made. Tell us how hot you would like it; the kitchen will cook it that way. Groups and functions welcome — call ahead and we will set the table for you.
