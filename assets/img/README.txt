Rules of thumb
- JPG, not PNG or HEIC (an iPhone HEIC will not show in a browser).
- 1200 pixels wide is plenty. Keep each file under 300 KB so the site stays fast.
  https://squoosh.app shrinks a photo in the browser, free.
- Crop to the shape above, or the edges get trimmed from the centre.

Where the current photos came from
----------------------------------
Pulled from Kairali's own public listing photos (Google / Yelp / Restaurant Guru
listing for Kairali South Indian Restaurant, Royal Oak) so the site would not
launch empty. They are your restaurant, but they are copies — if you have the
originals, drop them in over the top and the quality goes up.
