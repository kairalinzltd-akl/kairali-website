Kairali — photos
================

Put your photos in THIS folder, with these exact names. The pages pick them up
automatically — no code to change.

  hero.jpg     the big photo behind "Taste the heritage" on the home page
               1600 x 1000 or larger, landscape. A dish or the dining room both work.

  dish-1.jpg   gallery tile 1  — caption "Sadya on a banana leaf"
  dish-2.jpg   gallery tile 2  — caption "Meen curry"
  dish-3.jpg   gallery tile 3  — caption "Appam & stew"
  dish-4.jpg   gallery tile 4  — caption "Prawn moilee"
  dish-5.jpg   gallery tile 5  — caption "Parotta & ularthiyathu"
  dish-6.jpg   gallery tile 6  — caption "Payasam"

Rules of thumb
- JPG, not PNG or HEIC (iPhone HEIC will not show in a browser).
- 1200 pixels wide is plenty. Crop to 4:3 for the tiles, or they get trimmed.
- Keep each file under 300 KB so the site stays fast on a phone.
  https://squoosh.app will shrink a photo in the browser for free.
- Any missing photo simply disappears from the page — nothing breaks.
