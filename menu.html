The menu

Kerala on a plate

Coconut, curry leaf, mustard seed, tamarind and the day's fish. Everything is cooked to order — tell us if you want it mild, and tell us if you don't.

Tiffin Sadya & vegetarian From the coast Biryani & curries Sweet Drinks

01

Breakfast & tiffin

Served through the day. The tiffin plates are the food of a Kerala morning — eaten early, eaten often.

Appam with vegetable stew

Lace-edged rice pancakes with a gentle coconut stew

Puttu with kadala curry

Steamed rice and coconut, black chickpea curry

Idiyappam

String hoppers, with egg or curry

Masala dosa

Potato masala, sambar, three chutneys

Uzhunnu vada & sambar

Lentil doughnuts, crisp outside, soft within

Idli

Steamed rice cakes, podi and coconut chutney

02

Sadya & vegetarian

The sadya is the Kerala feast: rice on a banana leaf, a ring of small dishes around it. Ask us about a full sadya for a group — we need a day's notice.

Sadya on banana leafFeast

Rice, sambar, avial, thoran, kalan, pickle, pappadam, payasam

Avial

Mixed vegetables, coconut and yoghurt

Thoran of the day

Garden vegetable, grated coconut, curry leaf

Kalan

Yam and plantain in a peppered yoghurt gravy

Olan

Ash gourd and coconut milk, very mild

Pachadi

Cooling yoghurt with mustard and curry leaf

Sambar rice

Rice, lentils and vegetables, one bowl

03

From the Malabar coast

Kerala is a coastline before it is anything else. Ask what came in that morning — the fish changes with the boats.

Meen curry

Fish simmered in tamarind and coconut

Meen pollichathu

Fish wrapped in banana leaf, grilled with masala

Prawn moilee

Prawns in mild coconut milk, the Anglo-Indian way

Karimeen fry

Pearl spot, pan fried in a rice-flour crust

Koonthal roast

Squid roasted with coconut slivers and pepper

Fish fry

The day's fish, masala rubbed, fried to order

Biryani, beef & chicken

Thalassery biryani is the northern Kerala school: short-grain kaima rice, less oil, more perfume.

Thalassery chicken biryaniHouse

Kaima rice, fried onion, raita and pickle

Malabar mutton biryani

Slow-cooked mutton, with pappadam

Beef ularthiyathu

Dry-roasted beef, coconut and black pepper

Chicken mappas

Chicken in coconut milk, gently spiced

Nadan chicken curry

Country-style, roasted coconut masala

Kappa & kanji

Tapioca and rice gruel — the Kerala supper

Unnakkaya

Ela ada

Steamed rice parcel of coconut and jaggery
