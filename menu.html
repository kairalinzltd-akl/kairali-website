The menu

Kerala on a plate.

Coconut, curry leaf, mustard seed, tamarind and fresh fish. Every dish is cooked to order — tell us if you want it mild, and tell us if you don't.

  • Puttu with kadala curry Steamed rice and coconut, black chickpea curry
  • Idiyappam String hoppers with egg or curry
  • Masala dosa Potato masala, sambar, three chutneys
  • Uzhunnu vada & sambar Lentil doughnuts
  • Idli Steamed, with podi and coconut chutney

Sadya & Vegetarian

  • Avial Mixed vegetables, coconut and curd

  • Sambar rice With pappadam and pickle

The menu follows the season and the day's catch, so prices are confirmed at the counter. Call 09 213 3313 for today's dishes, or ask us when you book. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free dishes are marked on the printed menu — tell us about allergies and we will cook around them.
